# Firecracker Motor

Firecracker Motor is piloting Pay As You Drive cover and a set of limited policy offers. Support has heard that an ordinary customer received a rate reserved for a different audience. The claims team has also noticed an unfamiliar support status on the sign-in screen after a configuration rollout.

Use the fictional demo policy holder, Alex Morgan (`alex.morgan@example.com`, PIN `2468`). Investigate the employee saving, the VIP saving, and the claims-support rollout. Collect the three distinct flags associated with those findings. No real insurance policy, payment account, or cloud account is involved.
